What Is a Reading Time Calculator?

A reading time calculator estimates how long it may take to read an article, essay, story, or other text. It uses the number of words and your selected reading speed to calculate the result.

How Is Reading Time Calculated?

Reading time is calculated by dividing the total number of words by your reading speed in words per minute. The result is an estimate, because reading speed varies from person to person.

Who Can Use This Tool?

Students, teachers, bloggers, writers, and website owners can use this free tool to estimate the reading time of their text.
